Why the floorstarts to give way.

Do I have to undress?

No. You stay fully clothed the whole time, and nothing is inserted. You simply sit on the chair.

Does it hurt?

No. Most people feel a firm tapping and tightening with a mild tingle. Intensity rises gradually to a level that feels strong but tolerable, and you can lower it at any time.

How many sessions will I need?

A typical course is 6 sessions of 28 minutes, about twice a week for three weeks. Dr. Dooman confirms the right plan for you at your consult.

When will I see results?

Many patients notice first changes after 2 to 4 sessions. The full effect keeps building for 2 to 4 weeks after your course ends.

How long do results last?

Typical results last 6 to 12 months. Occasional maintenance visits help keep your pelvic floor strong.

Can men use it after prostate surgery?

Yes. Emsella can help with dribbling and leaking after prostate removal, once your surgeon has cleared you.

Who should not have Emsella?

Wait or skip it if you have a pacemaker, defibrillator or other implanted electronics, metal implants in the pelvis or hips, a copper IUD, a malignancy in or near the area, or untested urinary symptoms, an active UTI or blood in the urine. It is also not used during pregnancy.
